Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your iPhone. No ads. No accounts. No tracking.

Baby Noise Hero is an open-source iPhone app maintained by ProfLLM. This policy describes the app and its support website.

Audio stays on your phone

All sound analysis runs locally on your iPhone. The app does not transmit microphone audio, sound measurements, identifiers, or usage data to ProfLLM or anyone else. It has no backend, cloud processing, upload service, or remote account. Listening and finding work offline.

Temporary processing, no recordings

Microphone samples are analyzed in bounded, temporary memory and discarded. Audio recordings and session history are never saved. The short signal trace exists only during the current session, is cleared when listening stops, and is not written to a file or synchronized to iCloud.

No ads or tracking

The app has no advertising, advertising identifiers, accounts, analytics, profiling, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. There are no third-party app dependencies.

You control the microphone

Access is requested only after you tap Start listening. Stop ends capture. Leaving the foreground or changing the audio connection pauses capture and clears the session; restart requires your action. You can revoke microphone permission in iPhone Settings. There is no stored audio or account data for the app to delete.

Links and this website

Optional support, privacy, research, and Apple documentation links open in your browser only when tapped. No audio, measurements, or identifiers are attached. This support site has no scripts, analytics, embedded media, or tracking cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Linked websites and your browser have their own privacy policies.

Support and collaboration

GitHub handles information you choose to post in issues or contributions under its own policies. Issues are public: avoid recordings and personal information. Apple may separately provide diagnostics when you choose to share them through its platform or TestFlight; these are governed by Apple’s settings and policies.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact the maintainers through ProfLLM/BabyHero Issues. We will update this policy and its effective date if the app’s privacy practices change. Read the source privacy promise.
