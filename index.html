Investigate persistent high-pitched tones around your home, one sound at a time.

Baby Noise Hero helps parents and caregivers find distinct high-pitched tones and compare the same tone’s relative signal strength while moving slowly around a room.

Listen. Select. Compare.

  1. Tap Start listening and allow microphone access.
  2. Select a detected tone.
  3. Keep your grip and orientation steady, pause, and compare “Getting stronger” or “Getting weaker.”

The app focuses on persistent high-pitched tones within your iPhone microphone’s response. It cannot tell whether a baby hears a tone or finds it uncomfortable, assess hearing safety, or measure direction or distance. An empty result does not prove a room is free of high-pitched sounds.

Your sound stays with you

Analysis runs on your iPhone in temporary memory. No recordings, uploads, accounts, ads, analytics, or saved session history. Listening works offline and pauses when you leave the app.
