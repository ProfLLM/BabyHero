Support

Contact the maintainers

For help, questions, or bug reports, open an issue with the ProfLLM/BabyHero maintainers or read existing issues. A GitHub account is required to post.

Issues are public. Describe what happened and the steps to reproduce it. Do not post recordings, personal information, serial numbers, or device identifiers.

Start listening

Tap Start listening, then allow microphone access. Keep the built-in microphone uncovered and let the app listen for a few seconds. If access is off, use Open Settings from the alert to allow Microphone, then return and start again.

Follow one tone

Tap a detected tone to open the finder. Walk slowly with a steady grip and orientation, then pause to compare readings. “Getting stronger,” “Getting weaker,” and “About the same” compare the selected tone over time. A louder tone at another frequency does not change your selection.

No tone or signal lost?

“No clear tone detected” means no persistent tone met the detector’s criteria. “Signal lost” means the selected tone is no longer distinct. Neither result certifies a quiet or safe room. Short chirps, faint tones, broadband noise, and sounds beyond the microphone’s response may be missed.

Listening paused?

Leaving the app, locking the phone, interruptions, and audio connection changes pause listening and clear readings. Tap Start again for a fresh comparison. Only the built-in microphone is supported. Tap Stop listening to end capture.

Understand the limits

The search band is roughly 8–20 kHz, limited by the actual input format and microphone response. Sensitivity varies by phone, case, grip, and room. Reflections can change or reverse a strength trend. Readings are relative digital signal levels, not calibrated sound-pressure levels, distance, or direction.

The app cannot determine infant hearing, discomfort, or hearing safety. It does not measure general noise exposure or play test tones.

Privacy and deletion

Everything runs locally, including in airplane mode. Stop clears readings; audio is never recorded or uploaded, and there is no saved history or account to delete. You can revoke microphone access in iPhone Settings. Read the complete privacy policy.
